# Reachability Analysis Based Localization of Limit Cycle in Benchmark Planar Oscillators

Oscillatory dynamics are fundamental to systems in engineering, physics, and biology. In planar systems, the Poincar\'e-Bendixson theorem provides a geometric criterion for the existence of periodic orbits. Various analytical and geometrical approaches have been used for higher-order systems to obtain conservative and large region of existence of oscillatory behaviour. However localization of the limit cycle trajectory in these regions is desirable. In this work, we propose a interval-based reachability analysis to localize the limit cycle trajectory in the region of existence. Instead of propagating individual trajectories, we evolve hyperrectangular sets of initial conditions forward in time and examine the geometric relationship between initial and reachable sets to characterize oscillatory behavior. As a proof of concept, the framework is applied to benchmark planar oscillators like  the Van der Pol oscillator and the Glycolysis model, demonstrating feasibility and establishing a computational foundation for future extensions to higher-dimensional nonlinear oscillators.
