# Localization of Limit Cycle in Benchmark Planar Oscillators: An Interval-Based Reachability Analysis Approach

Oscillatory dynamics are fundamental to systems in engineering, physics, and biology. In planar systems, the Poincar\'e-Bendixson theorem provides a geometric criterion for the existence of periodic orbits. Various analytical and geometrical approaches have been used for higher-order systems to obtain conservative and often large regions of existence of oscillatory behaviour. However, localization of the limit cycle trajectory in these regions is desirable. In this work, we test an interval-based Reachability Analysis to localize the limit cycle trajectory in the region of existence. Instead of propagating individual trajectories, we evolve hyperrectangular sets of initial conditions forward in time and examine the geometric relationship between initial and reachable sets to characterize oscillatory behavior. As a proof of concept, the framework is applied to various benchmark planar oscillators like  the Van der Pol oscillator, the Glycolysis model, and the Stuart-Landau oscillators demonstrating feasibility and establishing a foundation for comprehensive extensions to higher-dimensional nonlinear oscillators.
